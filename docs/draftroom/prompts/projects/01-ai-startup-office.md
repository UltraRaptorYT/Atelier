# AI Startup Office — A Place to Think Together

Design the interior and outdoor threshold of a 40-person AI startup in one-north, Singapore. This is a hypothetical site and shell, not a surveyed property; all envelopes below are client scenario inputs.

SITE AND FIXED SHELL
A flat 40 m east–west × 30 m north–south plot: 1,200 m². An existing single-storey, 30 m × 24 m shell provides exactly 720 m² enclosed GFA, leaving 480 m² outside. Center the shell on the plot. Main entrance and garden face south; servicing is from the north. Clear internal height is 3.4 m. Keep the perimeter, a conceptual 6 m column grid, and the northeast 8 m × 9 m service core. Its 72 m² contains 32 m² toilets and 40 m² IT/storage. Do not add floors or expand the shell.

PROGRAM AND AREA BUDGET
Provide 40 permanent workstations, four enclosed one-person focus rooms, four phone booths, two 6-person meeting rooms, one 12-person boardroom, a central social hub, pantry, reception, storage, and existing services. Meeting/event seats do not replace the 40 workstations.
Working allocations, totaling 720 m²: workstations 220; focus rooms 40; meeting rooms 76; phone booths 12; social hub 100; pantry 45; reception 25; IT/storage 40; toilets 32; circulation and walls 130. Rebalance the flexible allocations while preserving the fixed core and total. Reserve at least 120 m² of the outdoor area for a shaded staff garden.

HARD REQUIREMENTS
All 40 staff must have identifiable desks. Quiet rooms must be reachable without crossing the pantry or social/event zone. Reception must lead visitors to meeting rooms without taking them through the main desk area. Keep a continuous main circulation route; use 1.5 m clear width as this brief's concept target, not a compliance claim.

VALUE AND VIBE
Deliver a place where intense individual work and spontaneous collaboration can coexist. Futuristic but warm: daylight, timber tones, muted greens, visible shared projects, and flexible social furniture. Avoid a gaming-office aesthetic, neon branding, exposed visual clutter, and endless open-plan desks. The social hub should feel like the company's living room, not a corridor.

SHOW AND EVALUATE
Show a dimensioned concept plan, furniture counts, a quiet-to-active diagram, entry/social-hub view, and focus-wing view. Explain staff, guest, and service routes. Compare V1 and V2 on acoustic separation intent, desk count, area balance, and preservation of the shared center. Report unverified capacity, acoustic, or accessibility claims as unknown.
