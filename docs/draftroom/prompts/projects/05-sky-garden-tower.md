# Sky Garden Tower — A Good Neighbor at Forty Storeys

Design a contemporary mixed-use office tower on a hypothetical urban site in Tanjong Pagar, Singapore. The parcel, envelope, and floor allocations are fictional client inputs, not real development rights.

SITE AND ENVELOPE
Flat 70 m east–west × 60 m north–south plot: 4,200 m². Assume streets north and west, a pedestrian link south, and service access east. Exactly 40 above-ground storeys, excluding roof plant; maximum concept height 180 m including plant. Covered ground footprint must not exceed 2,600 m². Preserve at least 1,000 m² of publicly accessible ground-level plaza/pedestrian space. Target approximately 41,150 m² enclosed GFA; hard cap 44,000 m². Below-ground parking is outside this concept scope.

PROGRAM AND AREA BUDGET
Floors 1–3: retail, public amenities, lobby, services, and podium circulation at 2,500 m² enclosed GFA each, totaling 7,500 m².
Floors 4–20, 22–34, and 36–40: 35 office floors at approximately 950 m² enclosed GFA each, totaling 33,250 m². Each typical office plate must include its core, services, walls, and circulation, with a working goal of 650 m² usable office space.
Floors 21 and 35: two sky-garden levels, each with a 950 m² plate comprising 750 m² open garden and 200 m² enclosed support. Their combined enclosed GFA is 400 m². Total concept GFA: 41,150 m². Do not count open garden area as enclosed GFA or add these levels above the 40-storey total.

HARD REQUIREMENTS
Make both sky gardens reachable from the shared lift lobby; at least the lower garden must have a proposed public visitor route and access-control boundary. Keep the office lobby distinct from retail circulation. Separate servicing from the main north/west pedestrian arrival. Reserve continuous vertical core space throughout the tower.

VALUE AND VIBE
Deliver a professional, durable commercial building with a humane ground plane and memorable planted pauses in its height. Aim for calm proportions, a coherent façade rhythm, shaded arrival, and a restrained crown. The gardens should be usable shared places, not thin green stripes in a rendering. Avoid arbitrary twisting, oversized logos, or a blank podium wall against the street.

SHOW AND EVALUATE
Show the site/podium plan, typical office plate, both garden plans, numbered floor-stack diagram, street view, and full-height massing. Reconcile 3 podium + 35 office + 2 garden levels = 40. Check GFA, core continuity, open-space allocation, and visitor/service routes. Treat lift performance, structural viability, wind, fire, accessibility, and planning compliance as unverified concept questions.
