# Pikachu House — Playfulness Without a Costume

Design a two-storey family house on a hypothetical infill plot in Joo Chiat, Singapore. The site and envelope are fictional design inputs, not an identified property or a statement of local planning rules.

SITE AND ENVELOPE
Flat 20 m east–west × 30 m north–south plot: 600 m². Assume a neighborhood street along the south, neighboring houses east and west, and a quieter garden edge to the north. Use a concept-only buildable envelope at least 3 m from the south/north boundaries and 2 m from east/west. Target 320 m² enclosed GFA, approximately 180 m² ground floor plus 140 m² upper floor; hard cap 340 m² and two storeys. Covered footprint must not exceed 180 m². Keep at least 240 m² as planted garden, including a connected rear family garden. Provide one car space near the street.

PROGRAM AND AREA BUDGET
For two adults, one child, and an occasional overnight guest, provide three bedrooms: primary 28 m², child's 20 m², and ground-floor guest 18 m². Working allocations total 320 m²: bedrooms 66; bathrooms/powder 24; living/dining 50; kitchen 20; study 12; family lounge 28; utility/storage 20; circulation, stairs, and walls 100. Allocations may shift inside the GFA cap. Connect living/dining directly to a usable covered outdoor threshold and garden.

HARD REQUIREMENTS
Keep the ground-floor guest room and a nearby shower room accessible from the entry level without stairs. Preserve privacy from the two side neighbors. Keep bedrooms visually calmer than shared spaces. Do not place a Pikachu statue, giant face, ears pasted onto a generic box, or branded signage on the house.

VALUE AND VIBE
Translate Pikachu's energy into an angled roof silhouette, a lively sequence from entry to garden, selective warm-yellow surfaces, and a sense of surprise. Use pale timber and restrained neutral materials to balance the yellow. It should first read as a thoughtful tropical family home, with the reference discovered through form, light, and personality. Deliver daily delight for a child and long-term comfort for adults.

SHOW AND EVALUATE
Show the site plan, both floor plans, street perspective, and family-room-to-garden view. Explain at least three spatial translations of the inspiration. Check bedroom count, site coverage, garden area, GFA, and privacy intent. Compare V1 and V2 while preserving the playful roof/entry idea and direct garden connection. Any accessibility, structural, environmental, or statutory claim requires evidence beyond this concept.
