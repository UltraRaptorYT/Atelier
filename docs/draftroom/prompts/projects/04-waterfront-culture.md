# Waterfront Cultural Centre — A Shared Roof for the City

Design a performing arts and community cultural centre inspired by the spatial ambition of Singapore's Esplanade on a hypothetical waterfront site in Tanjong Rhu, Singapore. Do not copy its exact roof or façade. All site/envelope conditions are fictional client inputs.

SITE AND ENVELOPE
Flat 120 m east–west × 80 m north–south plot: 9,600 m². Assume waterfront and a public promenade along the south, arrival street to the north, and servicing from the northeast. Target 11,000 m² enclosed GFA; hard cap 12,000 m², three occupied storeys, and 35 m concept height. Covered footprint must not exceed 4,800 m². Keep a continuous public promenade at least 10 m wide along the full southern edge. Provide at least 2,000 m² of publicly usable outdoor space, including the 1,200 m² promenade strip; do not double-count it.

PROGRAM AND AREA BUDGET
Provide a 900-seat main hall and a 250-seat flexible black-box theatre. Working allocations total 11,000 m²: main hall with stage/backstage 2,600; black box with support 1,100; public foyer/exhibition 1,700; rehearsal/learning rooms 1,200; café/public amenities 600; administration/staff 400; shared building services, circulation, and walls 3,400. Include separate performer/service circulation and a loading route that does not cross the main public foyer.

HARD REQUIREMENTS
The foyer, café, and waterfront public route must remain usable without a performance ticket. Keep public waterfront access continuous during performances. Represent both halls as spatial volumes with plausible stage/support adjacencies, not only as labels on blocks. Treat seating targets as requirements to investigate, not verified safe capacities.

VALUE AND VIBE
Create a civic landmark that is useful on an ordinary weekday. Explore an expressive, climate-conscious roof that unifies the halls and shaded public space. Translate the Esplanade reference into filtered light, layered shelter, and a strong waterfront silhouette. Aim for an inviting daytime porch and a luminous evening gathering place. Avoid decorative spikes, two isolated boxes, or a ticketed barrier along the water.

SHOW AND EVALUATE
Show a site/public-access plan, program/section diagram, daytime waterfront view, evening lighting concept, and public versus backstage routes. Track GFA, footprint, outdoor area, and hall/support allocations. Let V2 improve access or massing without losing the shared-roof idea or public promenade. Acoustic, structural, fire, environmental, and statutory performance remain unverified until appropriate evidence exists.
